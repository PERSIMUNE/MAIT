<h2>From research data to interpretable models</h2>
<p>MAIT—the Medical Artificial Intelligence Toolbox—brings data preparation, model evaluation, and explanation into one Python workflow for tabular research data. This book walks through binary classification, survival analysis, and regression, with worked examples and guidance for interpreting the outputs.</p>
<p>For researchers working with clinical, biological, or other tabular datasets. Start with a tutorial, then use the manual to explore the methods and configuration options.</p>
